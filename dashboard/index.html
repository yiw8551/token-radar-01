How the ranking is derived

implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap / TVL ratio of this snapshot's own tokenized protocols in the same category (ratios clipped to 0.02–10). A category with fewer than 3 tokenized samples falls back to its family median, then to the global median. Nothing is priced from a list of names: change the snapshot and every multiple moves.
  • Momentum — the protocol's 7-day TVL change, clamped to −30%…+40%, scaled by 0.006: a factor of 0.82–1.24. Unreported change counts as neutral.
  • Maturity — 0.85 at listing, rising to 1.20 at four years tracked with no token: the longer a protocol has run tokenless, the more its record is worth.
  • Coverage — every protocol on the snapshot's watchlist is ranked, plus every other pre-token protocol that is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one whose token already trades. Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart, in the comparison table at the foot of the page, with their real market caps.
